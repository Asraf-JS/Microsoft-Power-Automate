## Copying an expression

Open [expressions.md](copy-paste/expressions.md) and find the section for your chapter. Hover over a grey box and click the copy icon in its top-right corner. Then paste it into the Power Automate expression editor.

Copying from the box is safer than copying from the PDF, which can add line breaks or change the quotation marks.

## Before you start

* Use only the fictional data in these files. Never put real names, email addresses or company documents into the practice resources.
* Keep your flows turned off until your trainer asks you to test them.
* Only send test emails to the addresses your trainer has approved.
